A Solid Capital Base and Dependable Platforms

Kalawa’s experienced banking team offers investment banking services to corporations on debt and equity offerings as well as share repurchase execution. 

Our capital markets professionals, traders and sales force have extensive transactional experience in a variety of market environments.  We serve companies in a broad range of industry sectors, including utilities, financial institutions, consumer products, industrials, media, telecommunications, technology, health care and energy. 

With a solid capital base and dependable sales and trading platforms, Kalawa is ideally positioned to underwrite and distribute a comprehensive array of fixed income and equity securities.

Debt Capital Markets

  • Investment Grade and High Yield
  • Secured and Unsecured
  • Fixed and Floating
  • Senior, Subordinated, Convertible and Preferred
  • Money Market Securities

Equity Capital Markets

  • Initial Public Offerings (IPOs)
  • Follow-On/ Secondary Offerings

Corporate Share Repurchase

  • Open market 10b-18 and 10b5-1 execution
  • Fully integrated with Kalawa’s experienced and reliable Equity Trading Team and technology
  • Close monitoring, market updates and accurate reporting and settlement

Senior management is actively engaged in every transaction, regardless of client size.
