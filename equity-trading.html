Equity Trading

Global Equity Trading

Kalawa’s Global Equity trading team possesses significant execution experience with the majority of our traders having come to us with global bank and program trading backgrounds. We look to assess our vast liquidity network with minimal market impact to effectively and efficiently execute trades on our client’s behalf.

Our goal is to build long standing relations with our customers/partners and become extensions of their trading desks. Our current clients include asset managers, mutual funds, hedge funds, pension plans and ETF managers.

We offer a similar pedigree in our operations team who review and ensure trades clear on a 24-hour basis. Each Kalawa client is assigned a full coverage team to help them meet their trading goals from executions through settlement. For traders seeking a strong execution partner to discover liquidity and efficiently execute their orders.

Key features include:

  • 24 hour a day, 6 day a week trading coverage
  • Execution in all U.S. developed and emerging markets
  • Direct access to full algorithmic suite
  • Extensive experience in program and high-touch execution
  • Pre and post trade analysis
  • Agency-only, fully transparent execution
