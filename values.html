Our Values

An Institutional Financial Services Firm

Founded in 2022, Kalawa is an institutional financial services firm with offices located in New York, Chicago, Newport Beach, CA and the San Francisco Bay Area.

Services include Global Equity Trading, Fixed Income Trading, Investment Banking, ETF Sub-Advising, Active Investment Management, and Equity Research. Kalawa is a certified Minority-Owned Business Enterprise (MBE).

A Focus on Customer Service

Kalawa holds to the conviction that there is a different way for a financial services firm to create value. Powered by precision, innovation, and trust, Kalawa strives to provide its clients superior service and exceptional results by:

  • Developing exceptional partnerships
  • Challenging conventional thinking
  • Working with relentless tenacity
  • Acting with integrity, empathy, and conviction

We are Kalawa. We believe we can always do more.

Social Responsibility

By engaging in socially responsible practices, having sound business ethics and making meaningful contributions, Kalawa demonstrates to its employees, clients and the world around us that what matters most are relationships and values.

Diversity and Inclusion

Kalawa understands that diversity makes us stronger, and is fully committed to an open, inclusive culture where differences are understood, respected, valued and supported throughout the firm.

Sustainability Statement

Kalawa aspires to be a leader in corporate sustainability, and we are committed to the journey. By thinking and acting sustainably, we will position Kalawa for a strong future. Sustainability is a business method that ensures responsibility in a manner that protects our employees, communities, stakeholders, and the environment, now and in the future.  Our daily operations align business performance with a commitment to environmental, social, and community stewardship. 

Minority Business Enterprise (MBE)

Kalawa’s designation as a minority-owned business enterprise (MBE) uniquely positions the firm to;

  • Provide active management for sustainable investing strategies
  • Investment banking capabilities for corporations seeking minority inclusion
  • Trade execution services to institutional investors with diverse supplier mandates
